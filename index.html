Free, and fair about it

OffAir is free. When your phone is online, a few ads help pay for new games, and they keep out of your way:

  • One banner on the home screen, clearly marked
  • A short ad between some rounds, never during play
  • An optional ad to double your coins, only if you tap it

Offline, there are no ads at all. Anonymous usage stats help us fix crashes and pick the Most Popular games; you can switch them off in Settings. Read the privacy policy.
