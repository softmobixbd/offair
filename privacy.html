Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This policy explains what the OffAir app does with information about you. OffAir is a collection of offline games for Android and iPhone, made by SoftMobiXBD. The short version comes first.

The short version

  • Your games stay on your phoneProgress, scores, settings and favourites never leave it. No account.
  • Offline means privateWith no connection, nothing is sent at all.
  • Ads, from Google AdMobWhen online, ads keep the app free, or remove them with one purchase. In the EU and UK you choose first.
  • Anonymous stats, your callWhich games are played, and crash reports. One switch turns them off.
  • We never sell your dataNo name, email, contacts, photos or precise location is ever asked for.

1Who we are

OffAir is made by SoftMobiXBD, an independent mobile game studio based in Bangladesh (“SoftMobiXBD”, “we”, “us”). We decide how the information described in this policy is used, and we answer for it.

Reach us at softmobixbd@gmail.com or +880 1644-426285. More about us: softmobixbd.github.io.

2Stored only on your phone

These stay on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else:

  • your progress: levels, scores, best times, stars and rounds played;
  • coins, experience points, badges, daily goals and streaks;
  • your settings: sound, music, language, theme and the rest;
  • your favourite games;
  • the player name you may type in (it is optional).

They leave your phone only if you export a backup file yourself, and that file goes wherever you choose to put it. Uninstalling the app, or Settings › Reset all progress, deletes them.

3Collected when your phone is online

OffAir plays offline. When your phone has a connection, these services may send information. Nothing below includes your player name, your contacts, photos or files, or your precise location.

Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

What
How the app is used: games opened, rounds started, finished or left, scores, stars, time played, favourites added or removed, words typed in the game search, badges unlocked and screens viewed; the app's language and theme. Firebase adds an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system and app version, and an approximate region worked out from the IP address.
Why
To see which games people enjoy, choose the Most Popular games shown in the app, and improve OffAir.
Kept
Up to 14 months, then deleted.
Your choice
Switch it off any time: Settings › Privacy › Share usage & crash reports. Analytics never reads the advertising ID.

Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

What
When the app crashes: technical details of the error, device model, operating system and app version, free memory and storage, the game and mode being played, and a crash-report identifier.
Why
To find and fix bugs.
Kept
90 days.
Your choice
The same switch as usage statistics.

Settings from our server (Google Firebase Remote Config)

The app downloads small settings such as the list of Most Popular games. This uses a random Firebase installation identifier and nothing about you.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

What
Your phone's advertising ID (on Android), IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads (views and taps). On iPhone, OffAir does not ask to track you, so the iOS advertising identifier is not used.
Why
To show ads, measure them, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud; and, only where you allow it, to personalise them.
Where ads show
One banner on the home screen, a short ad between some rounds (never during play) and an optional ad you can watch to double your coins. If you buy Remove ads, the banner and the ads between rounds stop; the optional ad stays.
Your choice
In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked before any ad is requested, and can change your answer in Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings.

Google acts as an independent controller for AdMob. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play or the App Store)

What
Only if you buy Remove ads: the store tells the app that the purchase went through, with its order number and a purchase token. Google Play or Apple takes the payment; your card and payment details never reach us.
Why
To remove the ads you paid to remove, confirm the purchase to the store, and bring it back on a new phone when you tap Restore purchase.
Kept
On your phone, until you uninstall the app (a reset of your progress keeps it). The store keeps its own record of your purchase under its own privacy policy.
Your choice
Buying is optional. The purchase is not part of your backup files.

4How we use information

  • To run the app and keep it working (crash reports).
  • To understand which games are played and improve them (usage statistics).
  • To show ads that keep OffAir free (advertising).
  • To remove ads for players who bought Remove ads (purchases).

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not try to work out who you are. Under some US state laws, showing personalised ads can count as "sharing" information; you can opt out with the ad choices above.

5Who receives it

Only Google, as the provider of Firebase and AdMob:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Privacy and security in Firebase
  • AdMob and personal data

If you buy Remove ads, the payment is between you and Google Play or Apple, under their own privacy policies.

We would disclose information only if the law requires it. Google may process information on servers outside your country, protected by its standard contractual clauses.

6Legal bases (EU and UK)

  • Consent for personalised advertising, asked through Google's consent message.
  • Legitimate interests for anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, which you can switch off, and for non-personalised ads that keep the app free.

7Children

OffAir is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for most audiences (rated PG). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app and want their information removed, contact us and we will delete what we can.

8Your choices and rights

  • Switch usage statistics and crash reports off in Settings › Privacy.
  • Change your ad choices in Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices (where shown), or in your phone's ad settings.
  • Delete everything on your phone by uninstalling the app or using Settings › Reset all progress.
  • Ask us to access or delete usage data linked to your app installation, object to its use, or ask any privacy question, by emailing us. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9Security

Information sent by the app travels encrypted (HTTPS). Everything stored on your phone is protected by your phone's own security.

10Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If the change is significant, the app will tell you too.
